### knowledge for Major Electrical Appliances work with power station:
1. Typically, you can use a portable power station as long as the power consumption of the device you want to use is within the AC output range of the power station.
For example, the maximum AC output power of the Explorer 1000 New is 1500W. If the power consumption of the device you want to use is within 1500W, you can use the Explorer 1000 New.
However, some machines require a higher starting power than their normal power consumption, such as refrigerators, air conditioners, heaters, and air pumps.

3. Therefore, if you want to use a device that requires starting power, you need to confirm its starting power and determine whether it is within the AC output range of the portable power station.

###  Specifications of DC Input Ports on Portable Power Stations

Specifications of DC Input Ports on Portable Power Stations
The DC input ports on portable power stations have three specifications: DC8020, DC7909, and USB-C.
Through the DC input port, you can connect to solar panel input and car cigarette lighter socket input. In other words, when charging the portable power station from a solar panel or a car's cigarette lighter socket, you need to go through the DC input port. In the "Power Station and Solar Panel Specification Table," the DC input port is divided into two ports: the solar panel input port and the cigarette lighter input port, but in reality, they are the same port. ### How to Connect Portable Power Stations and Solar Panels
If the plug on the solar panel connection cable matches the DC input port on the portable power station, they can be connected. By utilizing the two plugs on the solar panel connection cable, all Jackery solar panels can be connected to all models of Jackery portable power stations. Some products need to be connected through Jackery adapters, such as the DC7909-DC8020 adapter, DC8020-DC7909 adapter, or DC8020-USB-C adapter.
For example, to connect the Explorer 708 portable power station with the JS-100C solar panel, simply remove the DC plug from the solar panel connection cable and insert the DC7909 plug into the DC input port of the Explorer 708.
To connect the Explorer 1000 New portable power station with the JS-100F solar panel, simply remove the DC plug from the solar panel connection cable and insert it into the DC input port of the Explorer 1000 New.
To connect the Explorer 1000 Plus portable power station with the JS-100C solar panel, simply keep the DC plug on the solar panel connection cable and insert it into the DC input port of the Explorer 1000 Plus.
To connect the Explorer 708 portable power station with the SolarSaga 40W mini solar panel, you need to use the DC8020-DC7909 adapter. Remove the DC plug from the solar panel connection cable, connect the DC8020-DC7909 adapter, and then insert it into the DC input port of the Explorer 708.
To connect the Explorer 300 Plus portable power station with the SolarSaga 100W solar panel, you need to use the DC8020-USB-C adapter. Keep the DC plug on the solar panel connection cable, connect the DC8020-USB-C adapter, and then insert it into the DC input port of the Explorer 300 Plus.
